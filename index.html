Demondawn – vocals • Mayhem – guitars • Nick Grave – bass • Messiah Of Evil – drums
 

SKYCONQUEROR is a traditional heavy metal band founded in 1997 in the countryside near Münster. In their early years they released three demos - their first album HELLSTORY was released after more than a decade in spring 2008.

In the following years the band played live and developed new material. The second album UNDER THE PENTAGRAM was released in summer 2014.

SKYCONQUEROR has had a stable lineup since 2014. Nick Grave plays bass guitar, Mayhem plays lead and rhythm guitars, lead vocals are performed by Demondawn and drums are tormented by Messiah Of Evil.

SKYCONQUEROR has been rehearsing in Dortmund's Nordstadt district for quite some time. There they recorded their most recent output - the EP THE BLACK RAVEN - which was released in February 2023. THE BLACK RAVEN received positive reviews in publications including Rock Hard and Deaf Forever. Songs from the EP have also been well received by live audiences.

 

The release of THE BLACK RAVEN earned SKYCONQUEROR gigs at German Swordbrothers Festival and Spirit of Metal, where the band shared the stage with ATLANTEAN KODEX, TYGERS OF PAN TANG, TYTAN and KEV RIDDLES' BAPHOMET, among others.

SKYCONQUEROR is currently working on songs for its next release, most likely a full-length album. The band is also trying out new material live and actively seeking opportunities to perform.

SKYCONQUEROR plays traditional heavy metal inspired by the occult both lyrically and musically.
